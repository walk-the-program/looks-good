## 1. What the owner wants

- **Character and proficiency together.** Youch! brief: "clean, sleek, modern ... classed up, kind
  of minimalist, kind of Bauhaus ... a little neo-brutal pop ... a slight bit of character, but also
  very, very proficient." Later: "a little bit more modern, slightly more character."
- **Fun over bland.** NFELO: the old site "was fun and cool", the new one "so bland". Sticker Book
  won. Tracy Christmas: the loudest of three, Retro Special, won.
- **Structural ideas.** The owner's picks are often layouts and behaviours: a Mail-style two-pane
  history (Youch!), a toolbar that fades while typing, tools that expand upward from a corner
  button, a quick-create popover attached to the menu bar (OpenText).
- **A signature moment.** Youch!'s listening circle of periwinkle static, see-through, gone when not
  capturing: "it looks amazing."
- **Clear glass**, never frosted or milky, when glass is used at all.
- **Real, clickable HTML** when the preview cannot show the idea ("give it to me in HTML").

## 2. What the owner rejects

- Colour highlight bands behind titles or words.
- Frosted or milky glass.
- Monospace when asked for no mono; lowercased file names in a file app.
- Lollipop charts (an area graph replaced one).
- Live windows popping up during a flow; screenshot tools popping up windows.
- Fake "classified" or "restricted" labels next to the product name.
- Board art or patterns on app screens.
- "Simple" executed as empty grey boxes (Scent Box, below).

## 3. How the owner judges

By looking, at real size, with real content. Not by reading specs. They react to a whole screen
first ("cheap", "bland", "looks amazing"), then name one or two specifics. Reverted "harsh"
studio-grade reworks of styles they already liked: improve without stripping out what was liked.

Fonts the owner has locked: Geist (OpenText), Outfit headings with Inter text (Youch!). Built-in
styles the owner has called favourites: dystopian-luxury, old-web, pirate-broadcast, blob-tracking,
xerox, xerox-punk, acid-graphics, corporate-vandalism, radical-memphis, ascii-art, micrographics,
gorpcore, naive-doodle, bento-grid, mesh-gradient, soft-form, holo-iridescent, aurora-noir,
industrial-device, blueprint.

## 5. Adding to this file

After every round the owner reacts to, add one row to the log: the date, the app, the owner's words
quoted exactly, and the lesson in one sentence that would change a future direction. If the lesson
is new and general, also add it to section 1 or 2. Never paraphrase the owner inside quotes.
